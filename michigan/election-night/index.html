Data sources, update limitations and technical notes

Statewide cross-check: Open America JSON contract (Michigan covered; updated on the provider's schedule). Its browser endpoint is read independently and is never summed into precinct totals. A CORS or provider failure is labeled unavailable, not zero votes.

Precinct returns: Published county statements of votes cast and attributed precinct/counting-board records are imported into the normalized results feed only after verification. Not all 83 counties offer compatible machine-readable precinct feeds; the site checks its own published snapshot every 30 seconds after polls close, but new county files may arrive later.

Alternative services: Nominate.AI reports historical Michigan precinct coverage but is not connected to this site. AP Elections API and DDHQ Results API require verified access and terms.
