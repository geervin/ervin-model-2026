      06 / Methods and data quality

      How county votes are estimated

      The statewide target is taken directly from the selected Ervin Model source and correction mode. For local geographic shape, each county begins with a weighted average of its actual 2024 Senate (55%), 2022 governor (25%), and 2024 presidential (20%) Democratic-minus-Republican vote margins. We then apply a uniform state swing across all 83 counties, mathematically solving for the offset that makes the aggregated county projection match the selected statewide model margin. This is a transparent scenario translation, not a fitted ecological forecasting model.

      County turnout begins at 70% of its 2022 governor ballots + 30% of its 2024 Senate ballots. The turnout slider multiplies every county's total by the same factor, leaving statewide margin proportions approximately unchanged. Third-party share is anchored to the weighted historical contests, and county Democratic/Republican shares are bounded to valid ranges. The 2020 Senate and presidential county returns appear in the trajectory panel for context, but are not used in the base county allocation.

      Uncertainty: individual counties do not have independent 2026 survey estimates here. Uniform-swing forecasts cannot reflect local candidates, county turnout shocks, differential demographic change, or actual county-level uncertainty. The Ervin + historical calibration itself is experimental. Displayed votes are hypothetical and should never be presented as measured 2026 votes or precise 2026 county probabilities.
