How to read this modelWhat the uncertainty and adjustment settings mean
Uncertainty: 80% or 90%

The shaded range is a model-based scenario band around the adjusted polling margin. 90% makes the band wider than 80%. These are not statistically validated confidence intervals or chances of winning; a band crossing zero means either candidate could lead within that range.

Original Ervin

Applies the full historical state polling-error adjustment. A positive Republican error reduces the non-Republican polling lead; a negative error increases it. This assumes past error patterns carry forward.

Shrink small samples

Pulls historical error toward zero when the sample is thin, limiting how much a few past elections change the forecast. It is a cautious alternative, not a new polling average.

Ervin +

Uses historical 2016–2024 Senate and presidential errors with recency, office, regional/national partial pooling, plus guarded repeat-candidate comparisons. It is an experimental, backtested adjustment, not a measure of current polling momentum.

All modes start from the selected source's current polling margin. When sources overlap or use proxy figures, their estimates aren't independent. Different offices and ranked-choice elections may not be directly comparable.

How this Senate-control estimate works

These are experimental, uncalibrated scenario frequencies, not validated win probabilities. The model simulates adjusted polling leads in 17 races, shared national error, and state-specific errors; assumes the other 18 Senate elections are held by their current parties; starts from 53 Republican and 47 Democratic-aligned seats; and treats potential new independents in Nebraska, South Dakota and Montana separately rather than assuming they caucus with Democrats. A 50–50 tie favors Republicans because of the vice presidency. Results change with your polling source, adjustment, or uncertainty selection. Senate party balance · 2026 elections.

06   HOW THE MODEL WORKS

Methods & sources

Historical polling error shifts the selected source's margin. Ervin + uses pooled 2016–2024 Senate and presidential errors; intervals are scenarios, not calibrated win probabilities.

Read model report ↗
Methodology, limitations and downloadable research
Historical adjustment

Positive past D–R polling error reduces today's non-Republican lead. Original Ervin uses the archived state comparisons; Shrink small samples attenuates that shift; Ervin + partially pools 2016–2024 state-cycle observations with national and regional evidence and guarded returning-candidate comparisons.

Uncertainty & evidence

Model ranges are scenario bands rather than independently validated confidence intervals or win probabilities. Historical audit grades are legacy comparisons, not statistically calibrated race probabilities. Independent and ranked-choice elections need extra caution.

Polling source overlap

Different aggregators may include the same underlying surveys. Ervin's Take combines separately attributed publisher averages and excludes explicitly flagged proxy inputs, with weighted availability/freshness adjustments. An aggregator-of-aggregators is not additional independent evidence.
